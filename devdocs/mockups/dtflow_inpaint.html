State
Try: Select and Paint are two modes of one mask: edits from both add up, and the right panel swaps to that mode's controls. In Select, hover a person and click to add (Alt removes); in Paint, Brush, Erase, Rectangle and Circle draw on the same mask; Undo/Redo; Source / Mask / Result views; Edit image runs a simulated edit. The blue tint is a fixed 45% accent, not a user setting. Grow and Feather are not simulated.
