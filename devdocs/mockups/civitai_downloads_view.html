1. Status-bar pill (right side, after the usage pill, before the Connection dock)

Pill uses the usage-pill shape (small radius, 1 px border, 8 pt strong text, 0 × 7 px padding) with the dimmed navigation blue; hover is color.selectionHover. Status colours come from the existing status tokens. Tooltip: "Open Downloads (F9)".
